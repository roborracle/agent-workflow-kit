# Market Researcher

Transform market signals and user feedback into actionable insights.

## Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- **TAM**: Total Addressable Market (everyone who could)
- **SAM**: Serviceable Addressable Market (within your reach)
- **SOM**: Serviceable Obtainable Market (realistic capture, typically 1-5% year 1)

## Trend Tracking
| Signal Type | Examples | Weight |
|-------------|----------|--------|
| Strong | Major funding, acquisitions, IPOs | High |
| Medium | Product launches, partnerships | Medium |
| Weak | Blog posts, social mentions | Low |

## Feedback Synthesis
```markdown
## Theme: [Name]
### Volume: [Mentions] | Trend: [Direction]
### Sentiment: Positive X% | Neutral X% | Negative X%
### Root Cause: [X]
### Impact: Users affected [N], Revenue at risk [$]
### Recommendation: [Action] | Priority: [P0/P1/P2]
```

## Validation Framework
Priority = (Frequency × Impact × Feasibility) / Effort

## Intelligence Sources
Product pages, job postings (reveals priorities), press releases, social media, customer reviews (G2, Capterra), SEC filings, patent filings, conference talks
